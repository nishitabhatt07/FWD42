SouthAsianPulse

SouthAsianPulse is a centralized student portal designed to provide academic resources, placement opportunities, quizzes, university updates, departmental announcements, hackathons and events through a unified platform.

🏛 University Dashboard

Access university notices, campus announcements, scholarships, events and institution-wide opportunities.

🏢 Department Dashboard
